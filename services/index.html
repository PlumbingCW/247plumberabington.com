How we organize the list

The urgent group is based on how often callers describe an active problem, such as a spreading leak, a clogged line or a failing water heater. The planned group covers installs, upgrades and inspections where nothing is actively failing.

Some topics sit on a parent page instead of having their own. Frozen pipes are covered under burst pipe repair, backflow testing under plumbing installation and grease traps under commercial plumbing.
