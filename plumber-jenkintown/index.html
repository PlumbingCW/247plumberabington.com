A borough inside a township: who does what

The borough's own history page says Jenkintown was incorporated on December 8, 1874, when about 248 acres were taken from Abington Township. It covers roughly 0.58 square mile and sits along the Route 611 corridor, which is Old York Road. Our office is on that same road, directly north of the borough line, so the trip into town follows one familiar street.

The practical effect is paperwork. Jenkintown has its own Building Code Official at Borough Hall, 700 Summit Avenue, and the borough says only master licensed plumbers may apply for a plumbing permit. Abington Township handles permits just across the line, so a repair on one side of Greenwood Avenue can answer to a different office than the house across the street.

What breaks first in a house that old

Sewer laterals that fail the camera

A drain line that is many decades old can crack, sag or fill with roots. Slow drains in several fixtures at once, or a backup in the lowest drain, point toward the lateral and away from a single clogged sink.

Galvanized supply pipe

Rust builds up inside older steel pipe until flow drops and a threaded joint finally splits. Low pressure on the upper floor is the early warning.

Leaks inside row house party walls

In attached homes a slow leak can run into a neighbor's wall. Call early, because shared walls turn one repair into two conversations.

Basement water after heavy rain

Water at the floor drain or a wet wall after a storm is a drainage problem first. A plumber can check the drain, the sump and the pipes before anyone talks about waterproofing.

Aging water heaters in tight basements

A tank in a cramped utility corner is hard to see until the floor is wet. A rusty seam or a puddle means the tank is done and a replacement should be planned.

Before we arrive in your Jenkintown home

These five moves limit the damage in a small borough house or a rowhome, where water travels fast.

1Shut off the main

Look where the water line enters the basement, often close to the meter. Turn the valve clockwise until it stops, and tell the dispatcher if it will not move.

2Kill power near water

If water reaches an outlet, a panel or an appliance, leave that circuit alone unless you can switch the breaker off from dry ground.

3Clear the area

Lift rugs, move boxes and pull electronics away from the wet zone. In a rowhome, check whether water is crossing the shared wall.

4Smell gas? Leave first

If you smell gas, get everyone outside and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us. A plumber comes second.

5Call the line

Give the address, where the water is coming from and a number that will be answered. Clear details help the right plumber arrive with the right parts.

Your sewer lateral gets inspected when the house sells

Jenkintown enacted a Use and Occupancy ordinance in May 2017 that requires a video inspection of the sanitary sewer lateral when a property is sold. The borough's January 2020 overview reported 119 inspections, with 32 of them, about 27 percent, needing repair. That is roughly one in four houses, and the borough itself describes its housing stock as historic with aging infrastructure.

If you plan to sell, book the camera look before you list, so a surprise does not land in the middle of a contract. Borough Hall confirms the current certificate rules, and our sewer inspection service explains what the video shows. If it finds a break, a sewer line repair visit sets out the options.

Jenkintown water and sewer, in plain terms

Aqua Pennsylvania has published water-main work in the borough, so it appears to be the water supplier. For sewage the picture is less simple. The borough says its flow goes through Cheltenham Township, and Aqua acquired the Cheltenham system in 2019. Aqua's tariff lists a portion of Jenkintown in that service area.

Because of that, ask Borough Hall at 215-885-0700 which system your lateral joins before you assume who handles a main-line problem. Under Aqua's sewer tariff, the customer lateral is the customer's to maintain, so a blockage between house and main is usually your repair. Test your tap and ask Aqua about water quality, since hardness is not published in its report.
