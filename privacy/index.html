Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026. This page explains what we collect when you call or visit this site and how we use it.

The short version

This website has no forms, accounts or checkout. The main way we collect information is when you call (215) 714-6692.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

What we collect when you call

When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, service address, a description of the plumbing problem and the contents of the recorded call. We use this information to dispatch a plumber, to follow up on the job and to review call quality.

We may share the details needed for the job, such as your address and the problem description, with the plumber assigned to your call.

What we collect from this website

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. We use this kind of data to keep the site secure and working.

The site embeds Google Maps and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may receive technical information from your browser when the content loads, under their own privacy policies.

Your choices and contact

If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call. You can ask us about information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing contact@247plumberabington.com.

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Where our 24/7 dispatch covers

One line covers Abington Township and the towns around it, including Jenkintown, Glenside, Willow Grove, Elkins Park and Wyncote.
