Before we arrive at your Willow Grove address

These steps work in any house here and keep a bad night from getting worse.

1Shut off the main water

Turn the main valve clockwise until it stops. It is usually on the basement wall facing the street, near the meter. If it will not move, tell the dispatcher.

2Kill power near water

If water reaches an outlet, a panel or an appliance plug, stay out of it. Switch off the breaker only from dry footing, and leave the power alone if you would have to step through water.

3Clear the area

Lift rugs, boxes and electronics off a wet floor and keep children and pets upstairs. Stored paint and cleaners on a flooded basement floor are worth moving too.

4Smell gas? Leave first

If you smell gas near a water heater or furnace, walk out, then call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Call us afterward for the plumbing.

5Call the line

Dial (215) 714-6692 with your street, where the water is coming from and a number that will pick up. A short clear description lets the right plumber bring the right parts.

Signs your Willow Grove house needs a plumber tonight

A pit that is full and silent

If the sump pit is full and you hear no motor during a storm, the pump or its power is down. Move belongings off the floor and call.

Water around the base of the tank

A puddle at the bottom of a water heater means the tank has likely failed. Shut off its water and power, then call.

Floor drain gurgling or bubbling

A basement drain that gurgles when upstairs fixtures run points to a blockage on the way out. Stop running water and call.

Meter spinning with everything off

Aqua's own guidance is to read the meter before and after a two-hour window without water use. If the dial moves, you likely have a leak.

Rust-colored water at the tap

Brown water after a pressure change can mean older pipe is shedding scale. It is a prompt to have a plumber look at the line.
