Where the 24/7 line sends plumbers

Abington Township sits in Montgomery County just north of Philadelphia, and its borders wrap around several communities with their own names and ZIP codes. We cover all of them from one line, so you do not need to figure out which side of a municipal line your street is on.

Dedicated town pages

Towns with their own page

Jenkintown

Old houses, borough permits and a sewer lateral that gets inspected at sale: plumber service in Jenkintown is built around how plumbing actually fails in a small, old borough.

Glenside

Basement flooding along Tookany Creek, sewer backups and pipes from the 1930s: emergency plumbing in Glenside covers both the Abington and Cheltenham sides of the 19038 mailing area.

Willow Grove

Sump pumps that quit in a storm, water heaters leaking onto the basement slab and drains that back up: emergency plumbing in Willow Grove is dispatched from Abington at any hour.

Elkins Park

Basement water after a creek storm, a backed-up floor drain or a pipe split in an older house: emergency plumbing in Elkins Park is covered around the clock from our Abington base.

Wyncote

Old stone-and-stucco houses, a creek that fills basements, and a line that answers at 2 a.m.: emergency plumbing in Wyncote is covered from our Abington base.

Neighborhoods inside the township

These communities are inside Abington Township and are covered on the homepage and every service page rather than on separate town pages.

Roslyn and Crestmont

The township's plan places the Sandy Run floodplain along this stretch, so basement water and sump pump failures are worth planning for. See sump pump repair.

Rydal and Meadowbrook

The township plan credits the 1870s railroad with driving development in Rydal and Meadowbrook. Houses that old are the ones to have a plumber check for original galvanized or cast iron pipe.

Noble, McKinley and Ardsley

The township plan dates McKinley to about 1895 and Ardsley to a 1918 layout. Older sewer laterals here benefit from a sewer camera inspection.

Abington proper

The business address and the township building both use ZIP 19001, along Old York Road near Jefferson Abington Hospital.
