Before we arrive at your Wyncote house

Most of the damage in a plumbing emergency happens in the first half hour. These five moves limit it while the plumber is on the way.

1Close the main

Turn the main valve clockwise until it stops. In many older houses it sits on the front basement wall, and if it will not budge, tell the dispatcher.

2Kill power near water

If water is reaching an outlet, a panel or a basement sump cord, stay out of it. Switch off the breaker only from a dry spot.

3Clear the area

Carry boxes, rugs and electronics upstairs. Basement storage in a creek-side house is the first thing lost.

4Keep the drains alone

If a floor drain is bubbling or a toilet is backing up, stop running water and stop flushing. Pouring in chemicals makes the plumber's job harder.

5Call and describe it

Give the street, the cross street and where the water is coming from. A clear description lets the right tools go on the truck.

What Wyncote homes tend to call us about

Basement water after heavy rain

Some low blocks near Tookany Creek take on water, and a failed sump pump or a backed-up floor drain is the usual plumbing piece of that.

Old supply pipe that splits

Houses from the early 1900s often still have galvanized steel or early copper somewhere, and those are the lines that fail without warning.

Slow drains that turn into backups

Older cast iron and clay branch lines collect scale and roots, and a main line clog shows up first in the lowest drain.

Hidden leaks in thick walls

Plaster and stucco hide a slow leak for weeks. A rising water bill with nothing visibly wet is the clue.

Water heaters past their prime

A tank in a damp basement corner can rust through at the base. Water around it means shut the supply and call.

Why 1951 matters when a pipe lets go

The Census Bureau's 2020 to 2024 estimates put the median home in Wyncote at 1951 construction. Of about 1,229 housing units, 464 date from 1939 or earlier, and roughly 80 percent were built before 1960. A house from that period may carry galvanized supply pipe, cast iron drains and original shutoff valves, so a repair on one section deserves a look at the age of the pipe beside it.

That is also why we ask about the material before quoting. A patch on corroded galvanized pipe can leave the next joint to fail a few months later, and you are better off hearing that before the work than after. If several fixtures show weak flow or rusty water, a repiping assessment is worth discussing once the emergency is over.

Sewer backups: Aqua runs the Cheltenham system

Aqua Pennsylvania Wastewater acquired Cheltenham Township's sewer system in December 2019, according to Jenkintown Borough, and has operated it since. Aqua's tariff defines the customer service lateral as the connecting pipe the customer owns. That means a clog between your house and the main in the street can be your repair, and a clog in the main belongs to Aqua.

If a backup hits, call us and also ring the customer line printed on your Aqua bill, which Aqua lists as 877-987-2782. We can clear your house lines and camera the lateral, and the camera picture shows which side of the connection the blockage sits on. Ask Aqua or the township how they want reports handled if the problem is the main.
